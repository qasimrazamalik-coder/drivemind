Temporary files

Leftovers from downloads, installers, Office and builds. Deletes go to a 30-day quarantine first.

Temp junk rules

Large files

The biggest files in your folders, with when you last used them. Pick what to let go: it goes to quarantine first, so you have 30 days to change your mind. DriveMind never removes these on its own.

Press “Find large files”. It looks in Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Videos, Music and your project folders.

Projects & dev

Duplicates (checked by content), old project dependencies, idle projects to archive, and developer data that fills C:.

Duplicate files

Keeps the copy in the most organized place; the rest go to quarantine.

Old project dependencies

node_modules, .venv, .next, .gradle… each restorable with one command. Build outputs go to quarantine.

Archive idle projects

Projects untouched for 90+ days are zipped with their full git history. Anything uncommitted or not pushed always asks you first; the original stays in quarantine.

Developer data on C:

WSL, Docker, Android emulators and SDK, Gradle, Ollama models and Steam games — sizes and the safe way to move each.
